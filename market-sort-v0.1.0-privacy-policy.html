Applies to: Android version 0.1.0 (version code 1)
Last updated: 12 September 2026
Developer: GROOT Games Teknoloji A.Ş. (Groot Games)

English

What this version stores

Version 0.1.0 stores your level, earned coins, progress and upgrade counters, owned and selected shop customizations, language, sound and volume, haptic, reduced-motion and relaxed-mode settings in the app’s local storage. This lets the game remember progress and preferences. This version does not provide an account or sign-in.

Gameplay events

During play, the game keeps a temporary in-memory list of up to 64 gameplay events, such as level starts, sorting results and earned-coin upgrades. These events do not include names, contact details, account identifiers or advertising identifiers. Version 0.1.0 has no implemented analytics transmitter, cloud-save service or advertising service that sends this list to Groot Games or another service.

Advertising and purchases

Version 0.1.0 has no in-game advertisements and no real-money in-app purchases. Shop purchases use coins earned by playing; they are not payments. Any future version that adds advertising, billing, analytics or another data-transmitting service will receive updated disclosures before that version is released.

Your local data

The game does not set an automatic expiry period for local progress and settings. Android’s app-storage controls can clear the game’s local data, which removes progress and selected settings on that installation. This does not promise deletion of an operating-system backup or an email that you separately send to us. This version does not offer a Groot Games cloud account or cloud-save restoration service.

Contact

For questions about this game, email grootgamestudios@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information. An email you choose to send is a separate communication; it is not data automatically transmitted by the game.

Oynanış olayları

Oyun sırasında bölüm başlangıcı, ürün ayırma sonucu ve oyun içi coin ile alınan yükseltmeler gibi en fazla 64 oynanış olayı geçici olarak bellekte tutulur. 0.1.0 sürümünde oyun içi reklam veya gerçek parayla uygulama içi satın alma bulunmaz. Mağaza içi satın alımlar oynayarak kazanılan coin’lerle yapılır; gerçek ödeme değildir. Reklam, ödeme, analitik veya veri ileten başka bir hizmet ekleyen gelecekteki her sürüm, yayınlanmadan önce güncellenmiş açıklamalara sahip olur.

Android’in uygulama depolama denetimleriyle oyunun yerel verileri temizlenebilir; bu işlem o kurulumdaki ilerlemeyi ve seçilmiş ayarları kaldırır.
