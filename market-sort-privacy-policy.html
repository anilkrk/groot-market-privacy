Market Sort Grocery Tycoon Privacy Policy

Market Sort: Grocery Tycoon is developed by GROOT GAMES TEKNOLOJİ ANONİM ŞİRKETİ (Groot Games). This policy covers the Android game, including its advertising and purchase features. For privacy questions, contact grootgamestudios@gmail.com.

Progress and preferences on your device

The game stores levels, coins, upgrades, owned and selected cosmetics, language, volume, vibration, reduced-motion and relaxed-mode settings on your device. It uses these records to remember your progress and choices. A separate gameplay buffer keeps up to 64 recent events in memory. Advertising and store services handle additional information as described below.

Android's app-storage controls let you clear local progress and settings. Clearing them does not erase Google Play purchase records, data already processed by an external service or operating-system backups. Store purchase history can be used to restore eligible purchased items.

Rewarded advertisements and Google

The game uses Google Mobile Ads to provide rewarded advertisements. Its SDK collects and shares IP addresses, advertising and other device identifiers, interaction information and diagnostic data with Google for advertising, measurement and abuse prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. Some identifiers and features depend on device settings, service configuration and privacy choices. Google describes its processing and storage practices in its Privacy Policy.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to update consent information and present required messages before requesting advertisements. Where applicable, the advertising privacy-options control lets you revisit your choices. Choosing whether to watch a rewarded advertisement is separate from the advertising service's consent and data-processing controls. Withdrawing consent does not automatically erase previously processed information.

Google Play purchases

The game offers non-consumable cosmetic purchases for its theme, shelves and checkout counter. Unity In-App Purchasing connects the game to Google Play billing. When the store connection is initialized, the game requests product information and existing purchases, including before you start a new purchase. This information supports product display, item access and restoration. The game stores item-unlock state locally.

Google Play processes payments and maintains its store transaction records. Payment-card information is entered through the store's payment flow, rather than a payment-card form in the game. Google's policy explains its handling of those records. Unity describes its services in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Support messages and privacy requests

If you contact Groot Games, we use your message and contact details to handle your request. We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Before acting on a request to access or delete developer-held personal data, we may ask for information proportionate to verifying your identity. Requests concerning records under our control can be sent to grootgamestudios@gmail.com. This process does not promise deletion of Google's own transaction records or copies outside our control.

Please do not send passwords or payment-card details. For information controlled by Google, its privacy policy describes the available controls and request routes. Deleting local game data does not submit a deletion request to Google or other services.

Cihazınızdaki ilerleme ve tercihler

Oyun; bölüm, coin, yükseltme, sahip olunan ve seçilen görsel özelleştirmeler, dil, ses düzeyi, titreşim, azaltılmış hareket ve rahat mod bilgilerini cihazınızda saklar. Bu kayıtlar ilerlemenizi ve seçimlerinizi hatırlamak için kullanılır. Ayrı bir oynanış tamponu, en fazla 64 yakın tarihli olayı bellekte tutar. Reklam ve mağaza hizmetleri, aşağıda açıklanan ek bilgileri işler.

Android'in uygulama depolama kontrollerinden yerel ilerleme ve ayarları temizleyebilirsiniz. Mağazadaki satın alma geçmişi, uygun satın alınmış öğeleri geri yüklemek için kullanılabilir.

Ödüllü reklamlar ve Google

Oyun, ödüllü reklamlar için Google Mobile Ads kullanır. Bu SDK; reklam sunumu, ölçüm ve kötüye kullanımın önlenmesi amacıyla IP adreslerini, reklam ve diğer cihaz tanımlayıcılarını, etkileşim ve tanılama bilgilerini toplar ve Google ile paylaşır. IP adresleri yaklaşık konumu gösterebilir. Bazı tanımlayıcılar ve özellikler; cihaz ayarlarına, hizmet yapılandırmasına ve gizlilik seçimlerine bağlıdır. Google, veri işleme ve saklama uygulamalarını Gizlilik Politikası içinde açıklar.

Oyun, reklam isteğinde bulunmadan önce rıza bilgilerini güncellemek ve gerekli mesajları göstermek için Google User Messaging Platform kullanır. Geçerli olduğu durumlarda, reklam gizlilik seçenekleri kontrolünden tercihlerinizi yeniden değerlendirebilirsiniz. Ödüllü reklam izleyip izlememe seçimi, reklam hizmetinin rıza ve veri işleme kontrollerinden ayrıdır. Rızanın geri çekilmesi, daha önce işlenen bilgileri kendiliğinden silmez.

Google Play satın almaları
